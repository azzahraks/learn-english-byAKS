Chunk 1

5-chunk routine

  1. Look at the chunk.
  2. Recall the meaning without translating word-by-word.
  3. Say or write one personal sentence.
  4. Use at least one chunk in today's speaking/writing.
  5. Review it from your error log later.

Complex Structure Lab

These structures are for controlled range—not for forcing “fancy English” into every sentence.
StructureModel
InversionRarely do students ask questions.
Not only...Not only did he forget his book, but he also lost his homework.
No sooner...No sooner had the bell rung than the students left.
Only after...Only after the results came out did we understand the problem.
CleftIt was the lack of practice that caused the problem.
What-clauseWhat students need is more speaking time.
Mixed conditionalIf I had studied abroad, I would be more fluent now.
Passive reportingIt is widely believed that practice makes fluency.

Revision checklist

  1. One main idea per paragraph?
  2. Replace repeated “and / but / so” with appropriate linking.
  3. Replace 5 generic words (good, bad, very, thing, make) where possible.
  4. Soften absolute claims when appropriate.
  5. Add only the most important errors to your Error Log.
